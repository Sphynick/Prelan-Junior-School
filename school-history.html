School History

Welcome to Prelan Junior School: the home of excellence

Established on 27th July 2021, Prelan Junior School opened its doors with a clear vision: to foster academic excellence within our community, address the need for quality early childhood education, and lay a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Since our establishment, we have continued to grow in both capacity and educational provision. Today, our school has expanded to include a fully operational Prelan Junior High School, with plans to welcome our Grade 8 learners in 2027. This growth reflects our commitment to providing continuity in learning and preparing our children for the next stages of their educational journey.

At Prelan, we believe that every child deserves an opportunity to discover their potential, develop good character, and flourish in a supportive learning environment.

Our Foundation

Where every great journey begins

We understand that the early years of a child's life are fundamental to future success. We integrate the child-centred principles of the Montessori approach with Kenya's Competency-Based Education (CBE) framework to nurture curiosity, creativity, independence, critical thinking, and practical problem-solving.

Our emphasis on strong literacy and numeracy skills, communication, social development, and emotional readiness ensures that children build the competencies they need before transitioning to Grade 1.

We do not simply prepare children for the next grade; we prepare them for the entire academic journey ahead. Our goal is to build a strong foundation that enables every learner to approach future learning with confidence, resilience, and a love for discovery.

Growing with Our Learners

Welcome to Prelan Junior High School

Our growth since 2021 is a testament to our vision, dedication, and commitment to educational excellence.

With Prelan Junior High School now operational and Grade 8 planned for 2027, we are strengthening our capacity to support learners as they progress through their academic journey.

We are committed to ensuring that our learners receive a well-rounded education that balances academic knowledge, practical competencies, technological awareness, moral values, and life skills.

Our Junior High programme provides opportunities for learners to explore their interests, develop their abilities, and gain the confidence needed to navigate an increasingly dynamic world.
