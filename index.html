★ Competency Based Curriculum (CBC)

Nurturing Every Learner's Potential Through CBC Excellence

Located in the serene hills of Nyathuna, Ngecha, Prelan Junior School provides a grounded, supportive environment where learners build real-life competencies, strong character and a lifelong passion for discovery.

  • CBC from PP1 to Grade 6: play-based Pre-Primary through learner-centred Primary.
  • Caring, qualified teachers who give every child individual attention.
  • Hot meals and school transport across Nyathuna and Ngecha.

Welcome to Prelan

Where Character Meets Academic Competence

At Prelan Junior School we believe every child in Nyathuna, Ngecha and the neighbouring communities deserves an inspiring foundation that sparks curiosity, confidence and a genuine sense of responsibility.

  • Competency-based learning that builds real skills, not just memory
  • Christian values, discipline and respect at the heart of school life
  • A safe, serene campus with meals and transport for families

Why Parents Trust Prelan

Academic competence, wholesome welfare, strong character and practical skills.

01 · Curriculum

CBC Curriculum Excellence

Competency-based learning that builds critical thinking, problem solving, digital skills and collaboration, preparing learners for Junior School.

02 · Teachers

Caring, Qualified Teachers

Patient teachers trained in CBC practice who observe, mentor and adapt lessons to every child's pace.

03 · Nutrition

Nutritious Hot Meals

A mid-morning snack and a hot lunch, prepared on campus in hygienic conditions to fuel growing learners.

04 · Safety

A Secure, Serene Campus

A calm, child-friendly compound away from busy roads, with managed school transport across Nyathuna and Ngecha.

05 · Talents

Vibrant Co-Curriculars

Scouts, music and drama, junior agriculture, ICT and sports let children discover and polish their gifts.

06 · Character

Life Skills & Integrity

Rooted in Christian values: respect, self-discipline, honesty and responsibility to the community.

A Simple 3-Step Admission Process

A transparent, welcoming journey for parents and learners.

Campus Visit & Inquiry

Book a visit online or call the office. Tour the grounds, meet our teachers and pick up the application details.

Learner Interaction

A friendly, age-appropriate assessment helps us understand your child's reading, numeracy and strengths for the right placement.

Apply & Enrol

Complete the online application, submit the required documents and secure your child's place by paying fees through M-Pesa.
