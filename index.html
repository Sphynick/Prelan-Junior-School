Competency Based Curriculum (CBC)

Nurturing Every Learner's Potential Through CBC Excellence

Located in the serene hills of Nyathuna, Ngecha, Prelan Junior School provides a grounded, supportive environment where learners build real-life competencies, strong character, and a lifelong passion for discovery.

  • ✓ State-Approved CBC Curriculum: Play-based Pre-Primary (PP1–PP2) through learner-centered Upper Primary (Grades 1–6).
  • ✓ Caring, Certified Educators: Low learner-to-teacher ratio guaranteeing individual attention and talent identification.
  • ✓ Safe Campus & Wholesome Care: Nutritious hot meals, reliable door-to-door school transport, and modern co-curricular activities.

Welcome to Prelan

Where Character Meets Academic Competence

At Prelan Junior School, we believe every child in Nyathuna, Ngecha, and neighboring communities deserves an inspiring foundation that sparks curiosity, confidence, and genuine moral responsibility.

📚

1. CBC Competencies & Literacy

We empower learners through hands-on inquiry, critical thinking, practical numeracy, digital literacy, and bilingual fluency in English and Kiswahili, aligned with Ministry of Education standards.

🎨

2. Character, Talents & Faith

Education goes beyond textbooks. We nurture music, creative arts, scouting, athletics, environmental awareness, and Christian ethical foundations to raise empathetic, disciplined citizens.

🛡️

3. Safe & Nurturing Environment

From fresh balanced meals prepared daily to clean sanitised facilities and trusted school bus transport across Ngecha and Nyathuna, we ensure children learn in safety and joyful comfort.

Thorough competency-based learning emphasizing critical thinking, problem solving, digital skills, and collaborative projects that prepare learners for Junior School transition.

02 / FACULTY

Caring & Qualified Teachers

Passionate, patient teachers trained in contemporary CBC pedagogical practices who observe, mentor, and tailor instruction to every child's individual pace.

03 / NUTRITION

Freshly prepared, balanced 10 o'clock mid-morning snacks and wholesome hot lunches prepared on campus in strictly hygienic conditions, fueling growing young bodies.

How to Join

Simple 3-Step Admission Process

We ensure an inviting, transparent enrolment journey for both parents and learners.

1

Campus Visit & Inquiry

Book a school visit online or contact our admissions office. Tour our grounds, meet our teachers, and pick up the official application pack.

2

Learner Interaction & Assessment

We conduct a friendly, age-appropriate assessment to understand your child's reading, numeracy, and developmental strengths for appropriate placement.

3

Registration & Enrolment

Submit your child's birth certificate copy, previous assessment report (for transfers), parent ID copy, and secure your place by paying termly fees via M-Pesa.
