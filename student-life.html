Student Life

Learning goes far beyond the classroom at Prelan.

Clubs & Talents

Every child has a gift. Our clubs help them find it.

  • Music
  • Ballet
  • Home Science & Cooking
  • Gardening & Environment
  • Scouts
  • Drama
  • ICT

Sports

Our annual Sports Day and regular games build teamwork, fitness and confidence. Every learner takes part, from the youngest runners to the oldest.

Facilities

  • School Buses
  • Computer Lab
  • Playing Field
  • School Kitchen

Meals & Transport

Nutritious hot meals

A mid-morning snack and hot lunch are prepared on campus in hygienic conditions.

School transport

Our branded school buses bring learners to and from school safely. Ask the office about your route.
