About Prelan Junior School

A caring CBC school in the hills of Nyathuna, Ngecha, where character and competence grow together.

Welcome

Nurturing every learner's potential

Prelan Junior School serves families in Nyathuna, Ngecha and the surrounding communities, from Pre-Primary (PP1) through Grade 6. We follow the Kenyan Competency Based Curriculum and pair it with strong values, caring teachers and a calm, safe campus.

We believe every child can thrive when learning is practical, encouragement is constant and discipline is rooted in respect. That is the Prelan way.

Our Three Core Pillars

The foundation of everything we do at Prelan.

📚

CBC Competencies & Literacy

Hands-on inquiry, critical thinking, practical numeracy, digital literacy and fluency in English and Kiswahili, in line with the Kenyan Competency Based Curriculum.

🎨

Character, Talents & Faith

Education goes beyond textbooks. We nurture music, creative arts, scouting, athletics, environmental care and Christian values to raise disciplined, empathetic citizens.

🛡️

A Safe, Nurturing Environment

Child-friendly grounds, clean facilities, freshly prepared meals and managed school transport across Ngecha and Nyathuna, so children learn in comfort and safety.

Why Choose Prelan

Six reasons parents trust us with their children's formative years.

01 · Curriculum

CBC Curriculum Excellence

Competency-based learning that builds critical thinking, problem solving, digital skills and collaboration, preparing learners for Junior School.

02 · Teachers

Caring, Qualified Teachers

Patient teachers trained in CBC practice who observe, mentor and adapt lessons to every child's pace.

03 · Nutrition

Nutritious Hot Meals

A mid-morning snack and a hot lunch, prepared on campus in hygienic conditions to fuel growing learners.

04 · Safety

A Secure, Serene Campus

A calm, child-friendly compound away from busy roads, with managed school transport across Nyathuna and Ngecha.

05 · Talents

Vibrant Co-Curriculars

Scouts, music and drama, junior agriculture, ICT and sports let children discover and polish their gifts.

06 · Character

Life Skills & Integrity

Rooted in Christian values: respect, self-discipline, honesty and responsibility to the community.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Talents and character are built in the garden, on the field and on stage.

Clubs & Agriculture

Junior agriculture and the 4-K Club teach responsibility and care for the environment.

Sports & Athletics

Football, athletics and games build fitness, teamwork and a healthy sense of competition.

Hands-on Learning

Practical projects and group work turn ideas into understanding.
