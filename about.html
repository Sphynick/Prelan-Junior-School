About Prelan Junior School

Nurturing Excellence. Building Character. Inspiring Greatness.

Learning by Doing

Our Functional Science Laboratory

At Prelan Junior School, we believe that meaningful learning happens when classroom knowledge meets practical experience.

Our functional science laboratory provides learners with opportunities to undertake practical activities that deepen their understanding of concepts taught in class. Through guided experiments, observation, investigation, and hands-on learning, children are encouraged to ask questions, test ideas, think critically, and discover how knowledge applies to real-life situations.

Our practical learning experiences support subjects including Integrated Science and Modern Agriculture, helping learners connect theory with everyday experiences. In Modern Agriculture, learners are introduced to the importance of agriculture, practical production skills, environmental awareness, and sustainable approaches to food production.

We want our children to do more than memorise facts. We want them to understand what they learn, apply their knowledge, develop curiosity, and become confident problem-solvers. At Prelan, we turn lessons into experiences and knowledge into practical skills.

Digital Literacy

Coding and computer skills

The future belongs to learners who can adapt, innovate, and use technology responsibly.

Prelan Junior School recognises the importance of equipping children with digital competencies from an early age. Through coding and computer skills training, we introduce our learners to technology, logical thinking, creativity, and the fundamentals of problem-solving in a digital environment.

These experiences help prepare our children for a world increasingly shaped by technology, automation, and artificial intelligence. Beyond learning how to operate computers, we encourage responsible digital practices, independent thinking, and the confidence to explore emerging technologies.

Our ambition is to nurture technologically aware learners who are prepared to embrace future opportunities and contribute meaningfully to society.

Beyond the Classroom

Discovering every child's potential

We believe that education extends far beyond books and examinations. Every child possesses unique talents, interests, and abilities that deserve to be identified, nurtured, and celebrated. Our vibrant co-curricular programme includes:

  • Swimming: Building water confidence, coordination, and essential life skills.
  • Ballet and Skating: Developing balance, creativity, discipline, and physical fitness.
  • Taekwondo: Instilling self-discipline, respect, focus, and confidence.
  • Chess: Strengthening strategic thinking, patience, and problem-solving, with opportunities to compete beyond the local level.
  • Music and Instrumental Training: Nurturing creativity, rhythm, and musical expression.
  • Scouting: Promoting leadership, teamwork, service, and responsibility.
  • Soccer and Table Tennis: Encouraging physical fitness, teamwork, sportsmanship, and healthy recreation.
  • Educational Trips and Tours: Broadening learners' horizons through educational experiences within Kenya and beyond its borders.

Every activity is an opportunity to learn, grow, build friendships, and develop lifelong skills.

Home Science Club

Raising independent and resourceful children

Through our Home Science Club, learners explore culinary skills, food preparation, kitchen hygiene, housekeeping, and responsible self-care.

We introduce these practical life skills at an early age to nurture independence, confidence, and responsibility. Our goal is to help children become resourceful individuals who can care for themselves and contribute positively to their homes and communities.

Prelan Press Club

Growing confident voices

Our Press Club empowers learners to communicate confidently, express their ideas, and engage thoughtfully with the world around them.

Through reporting, interviewing, photography, recording, and presentation, children develop communication, public speaking, teamwork, creativity, and media literacy. We nurture articulate, informed, and responsible young communicators who can confidently share their ideas and engage with peers in other institutions.

Christian Values

In a changing digital world

We are proudly a Christian-based learning institution, committed to nurturing children in an environment guided by faith, love, integrity, respect, compassion, and responsibility.

In today's rapidly evolving digital world, we recognise the importance of equipping children to use technology wisely while remaining grounded in strong moral values. We encourage our learners to embrace innovation with discernment, respect others, practise honesty, and make responsible choices both online and offline.

Our vision is to nurture a generation that is academically capable, spiritually grounded, morally upright, and ready to make a positive difference in society.

Our Teachers

The heart of our excellence

Behind every confident learner is a dedicated educator who believes in their potential.

We are proud of our team of qualified and professionally recognised teachers who dedicate their knowledge, time, energy, and compassion to nurturing young minds. We appreciate our educators for their professionalism, patience, commitment, and unwavering dedication to every child's growth. Their role goes beyond teaching lessons; they inspire, guide, mentor, and help learners discover what they can become.

To our teachers, thank you for making a difference, one child at a time.
