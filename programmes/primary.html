Primary School (Grades 1 – 6)

Grades 1 to 6: strong literacy and numeracy foundations, deeper inquiry and digital skills, and readiness for Junior Secondary.

About this level

In the lower grades learners become confident readers, writers and problem solvers. Lessons are practical and engaging, with plenty of group work, projects and encouragement from teachers who know each child.

What we focus on

  • Strong reading, writing and speaking in English and Kiswahili
  • Practical mathematics with real-life problem solving
  • Environmental and hygiene activities
  • Music, art and physical movement
  • Weekly co-curricular clubs

Two stages, one journey

  • Grades 1 – 3: confident reading, writing and number work through active projects and group discovery
  • Grades 4 – 6: science and technology investigations, ICT, agriculture projects, leadership and Grade 6 KPSEA readiness

CBE learning areas

  • English
  • Kiswahili
  • Mathematical Activities
  • Environmental Activities
  • Christian Religious Education
  • Science & Technology
  • Agriculture & Nutrition
  • Creative Arts
